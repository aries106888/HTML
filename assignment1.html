Tourism Terms

Safari
A journey where toursits travel through natural areas to observe wildife.
National Park
A protected area where wildife, plants and natural environments are preserved.
Game Reserve
An area set aside to protect wild animals and their habitats.
Toursit
A person who travels to another place for pleasure, exploration or learning.
