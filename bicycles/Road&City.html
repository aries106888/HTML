Road Bicycles

Road bicycles are designed mainly for paved surfaces. Their lightweight construction makes them suitable for travelling long distances.

Road cyclists often spend several hours riding on roads and therefore need comfortable equipment and good road awareness.

City Bicycles

City bicycles are useful for everyday transportation. They can help people travel short distances without depending on a car.

A city bicycle may have a comfortable seat, practical handlebars, lights and space for carrying small items.

Benefits of Cycling in Town

  • Helps people stay active
  • Can reduce transport costs
  • Does not require fuel
  • Can reduce traffic
  • Can be enjoyable

Choosing a Bicycle for Daily Travel

Consider the distance you travel, the condition of the roads, storage space and your comfort when selecting a bicycle.

The correct bicycle size is also important because it can make riding more comfortable and easier to control.
