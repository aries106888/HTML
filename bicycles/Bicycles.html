Different Types of Bicycles

Bicycles are designed for different activities and environments. Some bicycles are built for rough mountain trails while others are designed for smooth roads and city transportation.

🚵 Mountain Bicycle

Mountain bicycles are designed for riding on rough surfaces such as dirt roads, rocky paths and mountain trails.

They normally have strong frames, wide tyres and suspension systems that help riders handle uneven terrain.

🚴 Road Bicycle

Road bicycles are designed mainly for riding on paved roads. They are usually lightweight and have narrow tyres.

Road bicycles are popular with people who enjoy long-distance cycling and cycling competitions.

🚲 City Bicycle

City bicycles are designed for everyday transportation. They can be used for travelling to work, school, shops and other places around town.

🚵 BMX Bicycle

BMX bicycles are small and strong bicycles used for tricks, racing and recreational riding.

BMX riding is popular among young riders who enjoy performing jumps and bicycle tricks.

Which Bicycle Is Right for You?

Think about where you will ride, how often you will ride and what you want to use the bicycle for before making your choice.
