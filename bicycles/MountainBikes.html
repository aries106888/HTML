What is a Mountain Bicycle?

A mountain bicycle is designed for riding on rough and uneven surfaces. It can be used on dirt roads, forest paths, rocky trails and hills.

Features of Mountain Bicycles

  • Strong bicycle frame
  • Wide tyres
  • Strong brakes
  • Multiple gears
  • Suspension system
  • Durable wheels

Where Can You Ride?

Mountain bicycles can be ridden in many outdoor environments. Riders can explore forests, hills, countryside paths and other off-road areas.

Before riding on an unfamiliar trail, cyclists should understand the terrain and carry the necessary equipment.

Mountain Cycling Equipment

Mountain cyclists may use helmets, gloves, water bottles, protective clothing, repair kits and lights.

Carrying basic repair equipment is useful because a tyre can become damaged while riding on rough terrain.

🌄 Explore Responsibly

Keep natural areas clean, respect other trail users and avoid damaging plants and wildlife.
