The Big Five

Kenya is famous for its wildlife and safari experiences. The term "Big Five" traditionally refers to the lion, elephant, buffalo, leopard and rhinoceros.

Lions

Lions are one of the most famous animals found in Kenya. They live in grasslands and other suitable habitats. Visitors may see lions during game drives in national parks and reserves.

Elephants

Elephants are among the largest land animals in the world. Kenya has important elephant populations in places such as Amboseli and Tsavo.

Giraffes

Giraffes are easily recognised by their long necks and long legs. They feed mainly on leaves from trees and can be seen in many Kenyan wildlife areas.

Zebras

Zebras are known for their distinctive black and white stripes. They can often be seen in large groups on the open grasslands.

Rhinoceros

Rhinos are large mammals that are protected in several conservation areas in Kenya. Wildlife conservation efforts help protect them from threats such as poaching and habitat loss.

Birds

Kenya is also a great destination for bird watching. The country has many different bird species found in forests, wetlands, grasslands and coastal areas.

Going on Safari

A safari gives visitors an opportunity to observe wildlife in natural surroundings. Game drives are one of the common ways tourists explore Kenyan parks and reserves.

Visitors should respect animals, follow park rules and listen to instructions from trained guides while on safari.
